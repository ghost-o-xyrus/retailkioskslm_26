# Size guide

## Case size by wrist
| Wrist circumference | Suggested case diameter | Typical collections |
|---|---|---|
| Under 15 cm | 26 to 34 mm | Vivaah, Naksh, Solenne, Junior Scout |
| 15 to 17 cm | 34 to 41 mm | Vantage, Officeline, Concord, Bangle |
| Over 17 cm | 41 to 46 mm | Highpoint, Stadium, Seaward, Lumen Digital |

## Children
| Age | Suggested watch |
|---|---|
| 4 to 7 years | Analog learning dial, 30 to 34 mm, with a soft silicone or fabric strap (Junior Scout, Safari, Astro Kid) |
| 7 to 12 years | Digital with alarm and backlight, 32 to 36 mm (Firefly, Sea Buddies, Wee Ones) |

## Case thickness
- Under 8 mm: dress watches that slide under a shirt cuff (Wafer, Wisp, Solenne).
- 8 to 12 mm: everyday watches.
- Over 12 mm: sports and dive watches, which feel heavier on the wrist.

## Measuring your wrist
Wrap a strip of paper around the wrist just above the wrist bone, mark where it overlaps and measure the length with a ruler.
