# Service and repairs

| Service | Typical price | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Battery replacement (quartz) | Rs. 300 to Rs. 550 | 35 minutes in store |
| Bracelet resizing | Free for Veyra watches | 15 to 20 minutes |
| Strap replacement (leather) | Rs. 700 to Rs. 2,150 | Same day if in stock |
| Glass replacement (mineral) | Rs. 600 to Rs. 1,100 | 7 to 9 days |
| Automatic movement service | Rs. 4,200 to Rs. 7,200 | 4 to 5 weeks |
| Water-resistance test and reseal | Rs. 500 | 4 to 5 days |

- Automatic watches should be serviced every 4 to 5 years.
- Battery replacement within the battery warranty period is free.
- Book a service slot at any brand store or ask a staff member at the counter.
